OVERFLOW SCROLLING

Timing checks (no GUI required):
    swiftc discrete/Popup/OverflowScrollTiming.swift tests/OverflowScrollTimingTests.swift -o /tmp/discrete-overflow-scroll-tests
    /tmp/discrete-overflow-scroll-tests

Live checks after restarting discrete:
1. Use a command name longer than the overlay's available row width. It
   should show its beginning for 1.2 seconds, then move left at the configured
   UserSettings.overflowScrollRate (default 6 characters per second).
2. Watch a full loop: the next identical copy should arrive smoothly, then
   pause at its beginning again. There should be no visible jump or reversal.
3. Verify short labels stay still, key symbols don't move, and text remains
   clipped inside its own label/detail region and the panel's right padding.
4. Check long state/folder titles, context prompts, and search result names
   with long secondary locations. Short labels should retain their natural
   width so the secondary detail has room.
5. Navigate to another state or change the search query. New text should
   restart at its beginning. Change overflowScrollRate while visible: the
   new speed should apply and restart the pause. Zero/negative rates disable
   scrolling and restore normal truncation.
